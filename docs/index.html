bubble

A message bubble: framed conversational content with seven variants, alignment, grouping and reactions.

shadcn-dioxus add bubble
Default bubbles use the primary colour for the active user side of a chat.
Secondary bubbles are the standard neutral surface for assistant and conversation content.
Muted bubbles lower the emphasis for quiet system notes or supporting content.
Tinted bubbles use a softer primary tint when a primary fill is too strong.
Outline bubbles frame message content and give it a border.
Destructive bubbles flag errors or failed actions in a conversation.

Ghost bubbles work for assistant text and other content that should not be framed.

They span the full width of the container, which suits long answers and rich content.

message scroller
A chat transcript scroller: follows a streaming reply at the live edge, anchors new turns, opens saved threads without a flash, keeps your place when history loads, and offers a jump-to-latest button.
shadcn-dioxus add message_scroller
Can you walk me through how a streaming chat should scroll?
Sure. The short version: move the reader only when they asked to move.
What does that mean while a reply is arriving?
If you are at the live edge the new text stays in view. If you scroll away, it leaves you alone.
And when I send a new message?
Your message lands near the top with a peek of the previous turn, and the reply grows into the room below it.
Then what happens once the reply fills the screen?
Following takes over from the anchor, so the newest words stay visible until you scroll up.
How do I get back after scrolling up?
A jump button appears at the bottom. It is a real button, so the keyboard reaches it too.
Does it work for a saved conversation?
Yes: it opens at the end (or at your last message) without flashing the top first.
Scrollable: none
