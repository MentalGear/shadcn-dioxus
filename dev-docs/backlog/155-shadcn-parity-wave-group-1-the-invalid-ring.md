## Detail

shadcn's base registry moved on after our port, and our components lack props and parts a shadcn user reaches for first (a Toggle `variant`, a Dialog close button, `size` on form controls). Most items are a prop, a part or a CSS rule. Grouped by shared code, so each group is one lane with one file set. **1. Invalid ring (cross-cutting theme rule, M).** The `aria-invalid` ring: shadcn styles it on about 12 controls, ours on 4 (input, textarea, button, field). One shared rule in the theme fixes the whole class. **This is row 145's state recipe (focus, invalid, disabled) and the invalid half of row 133: build it once, inside that lane.** **2. Menu item parts (cross-cutting, M).** `Separator`, `Shortcut`, `Group`, `variant="destructive"` and `inset` on DropdownMenu, ContextMenu and Menubar. None of the three has them; they share `primitives/src/menu_item.rs`, so build once there and re-export. **3. Toggle and ToggleGroup (4 x S + M).** `variant="outline"` and `size` (default, sm, lg) on both, and `spacing` on ToggleGroup (M; decision A, APPROVED 2026-10-10). **4. Dialog (2 x S).** A built-in close button (`showCloseButton`, default on; our demo hand-writes a `.dx-dialog-close` button) and `DialogHeader`/`DialogFooter` (the layout parts exist for Sheet and Drawer, not Dialog). Row 141 (this round) is in the same stylesheets; merge it first. **5.
