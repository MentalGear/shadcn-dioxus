A sortable task list of 200 rows, each with a grip, a checkbox and a label. Drag a grip onto another row, or press Space on it, move with Up and Down, and press Space to drop (Escape cancels). Reverse and Shuffle reorder in one click, and the `item_key` switch shows what it is for: on, a task's checkbox, focus and measured height follow it to its new place; off, a row is its slot, so the checked box and focus stay at the same position and a different task appears there.
