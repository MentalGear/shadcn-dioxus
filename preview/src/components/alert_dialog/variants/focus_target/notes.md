`initial_focus` keeps the destructive action from taking the default focus. The line inside the panel reads the open's modality from the page and says where focus went.

- **Keyboard** (Enter or Space on the button): focus lands on Cancel, the safe choice, and its ring shows because rings follow the keyboard.
- **Mouse** (click): focus lands on the panel, not on a button. Nothing is ringed, since rings are keyboard-only; Tab moves to the first action and the ring appears.
- **Touch and pen**: focus lands on the panel, so nothing rings and no software keyboard is needed.
